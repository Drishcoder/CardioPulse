Preventive Cardiology

Evidence-Based Cardiovascular Health Guidelines

AHA / ACC Recommended Protocols
🥗

Cardioprotective Nutrition

Adopt a Mediterranean or DASH dietary pattern rich in leafy vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, and cold-water oily fish rich in omega-3 EPA/DHA.

  • Limit daily sodium intake to < 2,300 mg (optimally < 1,500 mg).
  • Eliminate industrial trans-fats and restrict saturated fats to < 6% of daily calories.
  • Incorporate soluble viscous fiber (oats, psyllium) to reduce circulating LDL-C.
🏃

Physical Conditioning

Structured physical activity enhances coronary collateralization, preserves endothelial nitric oxide production, and prevents adverse cardiac remodeling.

  • Perform at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise (e.g. brisk walking) weekly.
  • Alternatively, complete 75 minutes of vigorous aerobic activity (e.g. running, cycling).
  • Include moderate resistance/weight training 2 or more days per week.
🚭

Tobacco & Inhalation Cessation

Smoking induces profound acute coronary vasoconstriction, accelerates atherogenesis, and promotes acute platelet activation and thrombosis.

  • Within 24 hours of quitting, risk of acute myocardial infarction begins to decline.
  • Within 1 year, cardiovascular excess risk decreases by approximately 50%.
  • Avoid secondhand smoke and nicotine vaporizers, which trigger arterial stiffness.
🩺

Vascular Biomarker Targets

Strict surveillance and pharmacological control of systemic arterial pressures and circulating atherogenic apolipoprotein-B particles.

  • Optimal resting blood pressure target: < 120/80 mmHg.
  • Target LDL-C: < 100 mg/dL (or < 70 mg/dL for elevated baseline risk cohorts).
  • Maintain fasting blood glucose < 100 mg/dL and HbA1c < 5.7%.
🧘

Stress & Sleep Architecture

Chronic sympathetic overdrive and prolonged cortisol elevation promote microvascular dysfunction and systemic low-grade inflammation.

  • Prioritize 7 to 9 hours of uninterrupted restorative sleep nightly.
  • Screen for and manage obstructive sleep apnea, which causes nocturnal hypoxia.
  • Practice regular parasympathetic activation (diaphragmatic breathing, mindfulness).
🚨

Emergency Warning Signs

Recognize cardinal ischemic red-flag symptoms. Time is myocardium in acute coronary syndrome.

  • Chest pressure, squeezing, fullness, or central retrosternal aching lasting > 5 minutes.
  • Pain radiating into the left arm, shoulders, neck, jaw, or interscapular region.
  • Sudden shortness of breath with or without chest discomfort, cold sweats, or syncope.

Model & Cohort Architecture

CardioPulse utilizes an L2-regularized logistic regression classifier with sigmoidal log-odds calibration, trained and cross-validated on the comprehensive EarlyMed Cardiovascular Risk Cohort comprising 70,000 clinically documented patient records.

Clinical Intended Use & Disclaimers

CardioPulse is designed exclusively for educational, triage awareness, and preliminary epidemiological risk screening. It does not replace a definitive 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG), serum troponin assay, coronary CT angiography, or formal evaluation by a licensed cardiologist. If you or someone around you is experiencing acute chest pain or severe shortness of breath, immediately contact emergency medical services (102 / 911).

Technical Specification
  • Engine: Scikit-Learn Regularized Classifier
  • Optimization: L-BFGS Convergence with L2 Penalty
  • Calibration: Sigmoid Probability Response ($P = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$)
  • Dataset: EarlyMed Multi-Center Cardiovascular Dataset
  • 3D Engine: Three.js WebGL Anatomical Model
  • Visualization: Plotly.js Medical Analytics

Emergency Cardiology Action Plan

Immediate steps for suspected acute coronary syndrome

1
Call Emergency Services Immediately

Dial 911 or 102 for immediate paramedic dispatch with cardiac life support equipment.

2
Cease All Physical Exertion

Sit down in a comfortable position (preferably semi-upright) to reduce myocardial oxygen demand.

3
Chew Aspirin (If Advised & Not Allergic)

Chewing one adult 325 mg non-enteric aspirin inhibits immediate thromboxane-A2 platelet aggregation.

4
Unlock Front Door & Notify Close Contacts

Ensure first responders have immediate entry access without delay.
